## Rules

- Draws a rectangle by dragging. It becomes a path with 4 points.
- While drawing, a modifier key (Shift or Ctrl) forces the width and the height to be equal: a square.
- The panel has a visible option that links the width and the height, for the same result.
- **Corner radius**: set in the right-hand panel, for the whole rectangle.
- **Points**: in the [point editing mode](../05-point-editing-mode/README.md), the 4 points can be edited, each with its corner radius or its handles (see [Points and handles](../05-point-editing-mode/points-and-handles.md)). The rectangle can then become any shape.
- **Fill and border**: the ones every shape has (see [Fill and border](fill-and-border.md)).
- **Effects and mask**: the ones every shape has (see [Effects and masks](effects-and-masks.md)).
