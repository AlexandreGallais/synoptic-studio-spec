# Organisms

> [Home](../../README.md) › [Design system](../README.md) › Organisms

Components made of molecules and atoms that form a whole part of a screen. One folder per organism, with its sheet (`README.md`) and the table of its variants and states exported from Figma (`table.svg`). Only the organisms the screens of Metadata use are here (Product Owner, 2026-11-22).

Figma: page Organisms (`630:107`). The rules every component follows are in [Components](../04-components.md).
