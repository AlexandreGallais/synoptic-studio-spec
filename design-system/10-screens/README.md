# Screens

> [Home](../../README.md) › [Design system](../README.md) › Screens

The screens of the application as drawn in the Figma file, one folder per part, numbered in the order of the application. Each folder has one page per group of screens, with their ids and their SVG files in its folder `images`.

| Folder | Part | Figma page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [01 · Metadata](01-metadata/README.md) | Metadata | Metadata |

Symbols and Synoptics get their folders (`02-symbols`, `03-synoptics`) once their screens are drawn.
